What Apiary Log does

Apiary Log keeps a record of your hives and of every inspection: what you saw in the box, brood and stores frames, temperament, varroa drop, treatments and any honey taken. It is a notebook, not an advisor — it does not diagnose colony health or recommend treatments. Everything is stored on your iPhone and nothing is uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. The app works entirely offline. There is no sign-up, no login and no server.

Where is my data stored?

In the app's own storage on your device, in a single file. It is not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes your records — so export a copy first if you want to keep them.

How do I get my records out?

Open the Stats tab and tap Export CSV. A spreadsheet file is created that you can save to Files, mail to yourself, or open in Numbers or Excel.

Does the app remind me to inspect a hive?

No. Apiary Log does not request permission to send notifications. Instead the Hives list and the Stats tab flag hives that have not been inspected for longer than the number of days you set in About → Settings.

Does it tell me whether my colony is healthy, or what to treat?

No. It records what you enter. Varroa counts and treatments are stored exactly as typed, without interpretation. For colony health decisions follow your local beekeeping association guidance and, where relevant, the advice of a suitably qualified professional.

Can I edit or delete a record?

Yes. Tap any inspection in a hive to edit it, or swipe left to delete it. Deleting a hive also deletes its inspections.
