Apiary Log Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Apiary Log is an offline record-keeping app for beekeepers. Your data stays on your device.

Data collection

Apiary Log does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. It does not ask for your name, email address, location or any account credentials. There is no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service.

No account, no network

The app requires no account and makes no network requests of any kind. Hive and inspection records are written to a file inside the app's own storage on your device.

What you enter stays on the device

Hive details, inspection records, treatments and honey yields are stored locally. They are not backed up to any server we operate — we operate none. If you export a CSV file, that file is created in the app's temporary storage and shared only through iOS's own share sheet, to whichever destination you choose.

Permissions

The app requests no system permissions: no camera, no photo library, no location, no contacts, no notifications, no tracking authorisation.

Children

The app is a record-keeping tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete the app (long-press the icon → Remove App → Delete App) to remove all records permanently. There is no copy anywhere else.
